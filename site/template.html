How it's computed

Strive

Years = (bitcoin held × bitcoin price + cash + securities) ÷ one year of SATA dividends. One year of dividends is SATA shares × $100 stated amount (each share's face value) × the yearly rate.

Strategy

Years = (bitcoin held × bitcoin price + USD reserve) ÷ one year of dividends and interest, as Strategy publishes it.

Why this takes over.

isnt bitcoin too volatile for company cash?

depends how you hold it. long horizon and buying every month, hold it outright. need it to sit still and pay you, digital credit strips the swings off like a bond and hands you the yield.

arent treasuries the safer choice?

safer from swings, not from the printer. digital credit sits behind way more cash and bitcoin than it owes and the liquidity runs deep. more reward for barely more risk.

so what does a cfo actually buy?

STRC or SATA. bitcoin-backed digital credit, a coupon a t-bill cant touch, sitting on way more bitcoin and cash than it owes.
