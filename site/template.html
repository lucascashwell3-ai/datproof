why digital asset treasuries are going to take over

isn't bitcoin too volatile for company cash?

over four weeks, yes. over four years, it has beaten every asset a treasurer is allowed to hold. the question isn't "is it risky" — it's "what does sitting still cost."

aren't treasuries the safe choice?

safe from swings, and guaranteed to lose to the printer. 4% on a bill is a slow leak, not a return.

so what does a CFO actually buy?

STRC or SATA: a fixed 10%+ coupon, backed by a pile of bitcoin and cash several times larger than what's owed. the yield of the asset, the swings stripped off.
