DATproof

Privacy

Last updated: 28 Nov 2026

DATproof is a free, open-source data project. This page says what happens to your data when you visit datproof.com or use the DATproof plug-in.

Who runs it

DATproof, an open-source project. Its code is public on GitHub. Questions go to its issues page; security problems go through its security policy.

What DATproof collects

One thing: a visit count on the home page. No other page has the counter.

  • The counter is GoatCounter. Your browser sends it the page address, the page that sent you, the page title and your screen width. Like any server, it also sees your IP address and browser.
  • GoatCounter says it stores no IP addresses, no full browser details and no tracking ID, and keeps only totals: visits per page, the sites that sent them, browsers, systems, countries, languages and screen widths.

What DATproof does not collect

  • No accounts, no sign-up and no forms that send anything.
  • The “Your situation” calculator works out its numbers in your browser. What you type there is sent nowhere.
  • No cookies. The pages store nothing in your browser.
  • No ad trackers. DATproof does not sell or share data.

Outside services a visit touches

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub logs each visitor’s IP address for security. Its privacy statement says what it keeps.
  • Google Fonts and Fontshare serve the typefaces. Your browser sends them your IP address, browser details and the page address. Google says it sets no cookies for this and does not use it for ads (Google Fonts privacy).
  • GoatCounter counts home page visits (GoatCounter privacy).

The plug-in

The DATproof plug-in installs and runs on your own computer. It only downloads the public feed files from datproof.com (GitHub sees those requests as it sees a visit) and never sends the amounts or questions you give it. There is no hosted version.

Changes to this page

When this page changes, the date at the top changes. Every past version is public in the file’s history.
