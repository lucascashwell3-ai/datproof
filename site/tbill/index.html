The two questions every CFO asks

straight answers

Isn't bitcoin too volatile for company cash?

Holding BTC outright is volatile — that's why this page isn't about holding BTC outright. STRC and SATA strip the volatility off: you hold a fixed-dollar claim that pays a fixed-dollar cash rate, and the bitcoin sits underneath as collateral, not as your position. BTC could fall by half and Strategy would still be sitting on roughly $33B of bitcoin plus its $4.8B cash reserve. The volatility is the cushion's problem before it is ever yours.

Then what's the catch vs. a T-bill?

Three honest ones. The dividends are declared, not contractual — a board sets them (Strategy's has only raised; Strive's has held 13% since April). The price isn't par-stable yet — STRC has traded below its $100 anchor, near $95 this summer, which is why the rate mechanism exists and has ratcheted from 9% to 12%. And a corporate preferred is not the U.S. government. That's the trade: a marginal, measurable step out on risk — cushioned by the numbers above — for roughly three times the cash yield.
